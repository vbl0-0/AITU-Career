9.2 Dataset and matching baselines

The following is a next-stage protocol, not a completed experiment. Freeze 20 anonymized or synthetic profiles and 50 legally usable vacancies, using at least five candidates per evaluated profile. Use ten profiles for development and ten held-out profiles for testing; store IDs, source permissions, split membership and data hashes. This produces 500 development and 500 held-out profile–vacancy pairs. Where development and test share vacancies, the stated claim concerns new profiles rather than unseen vacancies.

9.3 Independent labels and evaluation metrics

Two raters independent of the matching implementation should assign relevance grades 0 (irrelevant), 1 (partly relevant) and 2 (suitable), without seeing algorithm scores. Store both original ratings and weighted Cohen's kappa before adjudication. Rater identities and actual dates remain unfilled until the work occurs. Tune aliases and semantic thresholds only on development data; use the same eligible candidates for all methods and break score ties by vacancy ID.

9.5 Validity threats

Validity threats include convenience sampling, self-reported skills, synthetic vacancies, language bias, tuning leakage, facilitator effects and a small profile sample. Mitigations are independent annotation, a held-out split, negative examples, fixed comparison conditions and retention of raw judgments and errors. Docker and the real embedding model were not exercised in this environment; controlled tests do not replace their evaluation.
