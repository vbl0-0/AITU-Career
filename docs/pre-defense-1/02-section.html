2. Evidence of Relevance

2.1 Sources of evidence

Relevance at this stage is grounded in the scientific literature and system comparison in Chapters 3–4. User interviews were optional and were not conducted; local demand and user-comprehension claims therefore remain hypotheses.

2.3 Further validation

Later validation may include anonymized discussions with graduates, career-center staff and recruiters. Record actual dates and consent, distinguish observations from interpretations, and link findings to requirement decisions.
